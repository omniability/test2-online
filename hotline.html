How it Works

Omniability delivers seamless 24/7 care through a straightforward four-step model.

Step 1: Streamlined Onboarding

Fill out a simple registration form to get started. A dedicated team member contacts you directly to guide you through setting up your personalized plan step-by-step.

Step 2: 24/7 Instant Access

Reach out whenever you need us. Get round-the-clock connection to trained support staff for urgent assistance, guidance, or simply a friendly conversation.

Step 3: Structured Check-Ins

Stay on track with routine scheduled calls from your dedicated team. We actively monitor your overall well-being, offer encouragement, and track your progress toward personal goals.

Step 4: Hands-On Daily Assistance

Receive practical day-to-day support tailored to your life, including medication reminders, weekly routine scheduling, and regular virtual companionship.

Eligibility

Services are available to NDIS participants across Australia whose personal goals align with the care model established during initial onboarding. The program is fully funded through Core Supports under the NDIS line item Assistance with Self-Care Activities

Predictable Core Support Funding

The program is fully funded through Core Supports under the NDIS line item Assistance with Self-Care Activities, utilizing a flat, predictable rate regardless of whether care takes place on weekdays, weekends, or public holidays.

Flexible Plan Management Options

We offer complete flexibility by accommodating participants under Plan Managed, Self-Managed, or NDIA Managed plan options.
