Services

At Omniability Support Services, we are dedicated to delivering person-centered, high-quality care that empowers participants to achieve their goals, build independence, and thrive in their communities.

Supported Independent Living (SIL)

Our SIL services focus on personal growth and life-skill development within a shared or independent living environment. We provide personalized, 24/7 assistance to help clients master everyday tasks—such as cooking balanced meals, maintaining a clean home, and managing daily schedules—building confidence and self-reliance every step of the way.

Experienced Care Independence

Specialist Disability Accomodation (SDA)

Omniability delivers purpose-built, highly accessible homes designed to empower individuals with significant or persistent disabilities to live with safety, dignity, and independence. Our modern residences incorporate state-of-the-art Assistive Technology and tailored physical features, supported by dedicated care teams to help every resident thrive in a secure community setting.

24/7 Care Dedicated Team

Personal Care and Daily Assistance

Omniability delivers compassionate, person-centered personal care tailored precisely to individual routines and preferences. Our trained support workers assist with essential daily activities—including personal hygiene, dressing, mobility, and medication management—ensuring every client receives respectful care that preserves their privacy and independence.

Social and Community Participation

We actively facilitate community connection by encouraging clients to explore their interests, build lasting relationships, and engage in local activities. From art workshops and recreational groups to local outings and social events, our tailored support helps individuals build meaningful social networks and feel truly connected to their community.

Support Coordination

Our expert Support Coordinators simplify the NDIS landscape, guiding participants through their plans to ensure they maximize every available resource. We work alongside you to connect with trusted service providers, optimize support funding, and build your overall capacity to manage your plan independently over time.
