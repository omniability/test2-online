Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Omniability Support Services Pty Ltd (“Omniability”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and disclose personal information when you contact us, use our website, submit an online enquiry or lead form, or enquire about our disability and NDIS-related services.

Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect personal information including:

  1. Your name;
  2. Telephone number;
  3. Email address;
  4. Information about the services or supports you are seeking;
  5. Information you provide when making an enquiry;
  6. Information relating to your NDIS plan or funding where relevant to providing our services; and
  7. Other information you voluntarily provide to us.

If you become a participant or proceed further with our services, we may need to collect additional information relevant to providing appropriate supports and services.

Some information may be sensitive information. Where applicable, we handle sensitive information with additional care and obtain consent where required.

Online Enquiries and Facebook or Instagram Lead Forms

When you submit an enquiry through our website or through an advertisement or lead form on Facebook or Instagram, we may receive the information you provide in that form.

This may include your name, phone number, email address and your responses to questions about the type of support or service you are looking for.

We may use this information to:

  1. respond to your enquiry;
  2. contact you about the service you have asked about;
  3. understand what assistance you may be looking for;
  4. determine whether our services may be suitable for you; and
  5. arrange further discussions where appropriate.

Submitting an enquiry does not require you to become an Omniability participant.

How We Collect Personal Information

We may collect personal information directly from you through:

  1. our website;
  2. online enquiry forms;
  3. Facebook and Instagram lead forms;
  4. telephone calls;
  5. emails;
  6. face-to-face interactions; and
  7. other correspondence with us.

Where appropriate, information may also be provided by an authorised representative, nominee, family member, referring organisation or other third party.

How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information to:

  1. respond to enquiries;
  2. provide and manage our services;
  3. understand individual needs and preferences;
  4. communicate with participants and prospective participants;
  5. coordinate and administer supports;
  6. manage our business operations;
  7. improve our services; and
  8. meet applicable legal, regulatory and reporting requirements.

We will not use sensitive information for unrelated purposes without appropriate consent or another lawful basis.

Disclosure of Personal Information

Where reasonably necessary, we may disclose personal information to:

  1. people or organisations involved in providing or administering services;
  2. authorised representatives or nominees;
  3. professional advisers;
  4. technology and service providers that assist us in operating our business;
  5. government or regulatory bodies where required or authorised; and
  6. other parties where you have authorised the disclosure or where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

Where you interact with our advertisements or forms through Facebook or Instagram, Meta may also process information in accordance with its own privacy practices.

Security and Storage

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss, unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

We use appropriate administrative, physical and technological safeguards, which may include restricted access, password protection, security software and limiting access to information to people who require it to perform their duties.

Accessing or Correcting Your Personal Information

You may contact us if you would like to request access to personal information we hold about you or ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date.

You may also contact us regarding consent you have previously provided, subject to any legal, regulatory or record-keeping obligations that may apply.

Privacy Concerns and Complaints

If you have a question or concern about how we collect, use, store or disclose your personal information, please contact us.

We will take reasonable steps to consider and respond to privacy concerns and complaints.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, practices or legal and regulatory requirements.

The current version will be published on our website.
